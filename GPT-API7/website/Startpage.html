Welcome to TriExtract

TriExtract helps you build and expand a Wikibase knowledge graph from your data. You can upload an existing ontology/export to continue from your current graph, or leave uploads empty to start from the default setup.

Quick start: upload your files (optional where indicated), then click Begin to continue.
